<p>This talk explores how a vibrant, idea-rich environment contributes to employee well-being and organizational success. Using examples from my experience at companies like Made.com, I discussed the key components that define a positive workplace culture such as values, leadership style, and employee engagement. The presentation emphasized the tangible benefits of a positive culture, including enhanced productivity, creativity, and employee health.</p>
